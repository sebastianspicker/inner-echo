# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Inner Echo is a public-first guided, client-only media lab for people who want a careful way to encounter and discuss metaphorical audiovisual representations of difficult internal experiences. The general public is the primary audience. Facilitators and educators, and people with lived experience who choose to use a reflective tool, are important secondary audiences.

The core job is to let someone begin a bounded, private session, choose or compose an experience frame, adjust its intensity and comfort controls, and stop immediately whenever they want. It must remain understandable without clinical knowledge or technical setup.

## Product Purpose

Inner Echo makes experiential metaphors discussable without presenting them as diagnoses, treatment, clinical simulation, or objective representations of a person. Camera, microphone, and rendering stay in the browser; the product asks for media access only through an explicit user action.

Success means a visitor can understand the framing and limits before starting, use the experience without surrendering control, and leave with no implication that the app assessed, stored, or transmitted anything about them.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, transparent, quietly expressive. The product should feel like a calm guided media lab: serious enough for a public educational setting, human enough for individual reflection, and never theatrical about distress.

## Anti-references

- A generic wellness app that substitutes soothing language for clear boundaries.
- A clinical dashboard that turns subjective experiences into diagnoses, scores, or patient data.
- AI-SaaS glassmorphism: translucent floating cards, decorative blur, glowing metrics, and implied automation.
- A developer console whose technical status overwhelms the guided experience.

## Design Principles

1. **Explain before immersion.** State the metaphorical, non-diagnostic frame and local-first boundary in plain language before requesting media permissions.
2. **Control is always nearby.** Keep intensity, Safe Mode, Reduced Motion, and Stop Everything direct, legible, and truthful.
3. **Guide without prescribing.** Use calm, concrete language that supports exploration without claiming expertise over the visitor's experience.
4. **Let expression live in the media, not the chrome.** The interface should be restrained so the optional audiovisual metaphor remains the intentional expressive layer.
5. **Make limits inspectable.** Evidence, privacy, permission state, and safety behaviour must be easy to find and accurately described.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public-alpha workflows target WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard-complete interaction, visible focus, clear semantic labels and status, and sufficient text and control contrast. Critical controls, including begin, stop, safety, consent, and drawer-close controls, must have a minimum 44 by 44 CSS-pixel target or an equivalently sized labelled hit area.

Reduced motion and sensory safeguards are product requirements, not appearance preferences. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`, provide the in-product Reduced Motion and Safe Mode controls, avoid flashes and abrupt sensory changes, and offer a clear non-immersive path before any media permission is granted.
